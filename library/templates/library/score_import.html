Import your library from a spreadsheet

Spreadsheets come in all shapes and sizes, and Precentor can't guess what yours means. To bring your library in, put it into Precentor's format first. It takes a few minutes and means the import just works.

  1. Download the template.
  2. Copy your details into it, one score per row. Only Title and Composer surname are needed; leave anything you don't have blank.
  3. Save it as a CSV. In Excel: Save As, then CSV UTF-8. In Google Sheets you can skip this: choose Share, set General access to Anyone with the link, and copy the link.
  4. Choose the file, or paste the sheet's link, below and press Import.

Nothing is final. Afterwards you'll see what was added and can keep it or remove the whole import in one go. Whatever you keep, you can edit any score and fill in what's missing at any time. Up to {{ max_rows|floatformat:"g" }} scores at a time.
